My Design Process

A structured approach to solving problems and creating impactful designs

1

Research & Discovery

Understanding user needs, business goals, and market context through interviews, surveys, and competitive analysis.

2

Define & Ideate

Synthesizing research findings into user personas, journey maps, and problem statements. Generating creative solutions through brainstorming.

3

Prototype & Test

Creating low to high fidelity prototypes and conducting usability tests to validate concepts and iterate based on feedback.

4

Refine & Implement

Polishing designs based on testing results, creating design systems, and collaborating with developers for seamless implementation.
